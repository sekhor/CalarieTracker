# Screenshots for PWA Manifest & Google Play

Place your app screenshots here. They are referenced in `manifest.webmanifest`
and shown in the Google Play install sheet / Chrome install dialog.

## Required files (390×844 px, PNG):

- `screenshot-dashboard.png`  — Dashboard view showing daily calorie ring
- `screenshot-scanner.png`    — AI Scanner view with food photo result
- `screenshot-log.png`        — Meal Log view with history list

## How to capture screenshots:

1. Open the app in Chrome on a phone (or use Chrome DevTools device emulation at 390×844)
2. Take a screenshot with your phone's screenshot button, or
   in DevTools: three-dot menu → "Capture screenshot"
3. Rename and place the PNG files in this folder
4. Rebuild and redeploy the frontend

## Minimum Google Play requirement:
- At least 2 screenshots
- Size: 320×568 minimum, 3840×3840 maximum
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 9:16 recommended
